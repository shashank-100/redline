============================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of July 28, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Vantorre Retail Group ("Customer") and Corvessa Labs, Inc. ("Vendor"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Affiliate" means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests.

"Change Order" means a written instrument executed by both parties modifying an Order Form or Statement of Work.

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, moral rights, and all other intellectual property rights, registered or unregistered, worldwide.

"Malicious Code" means viruses, worms, time bombs, and other code, files, or programs designed to disrupt, damage, or limit the operation of software or hardware.

"Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person contained in Customer Data.

"Subscription Term" means the period of authorized access to the Platform stated in the applicable Order Form.

"Support Hours" means the hours during which Vendor provides support, as set out in the Support Terms.

"Third-Party Materials" means software, data, or services provided by third parties and identified as such in the Documentation or an Order Form.

"Uptime Percentage" means the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the production Platform is available, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure Events.

"Usage Data" means technical logs, telemetry, and metadata generated by operation of the Platform, excluding the content of Customer Data.

2. PRICING COMMITMENTS
Vendor represents that the fees charged to Customer are, and during the term will remain, no less favorable than the fees charged to any other customer of Vendor for materially similar services. If Vendor offers more favorable pricing to any other customer, that pricing will apply retroactively to Customer.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR OWED BY CUSTOMER DURING THE 12 MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY, AND NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR INDIRECT DAMAGES. This limitation does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 15 (Confidentiality), Customer's payment obligations, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
As between the parties, Vendor owns the Platform, the Documentation, and all modifications, improvements, and derivative works of them, together with all intellectual property rights therein. Customer's rights are limited to the subscriptions and licenses expressly granted in this Agreement. Customer assigns to Vendor all right, title, and interest in feedback and suggestions concerning the Platform or the Services.

5. PUBLICITY
Vendor may not reference Customer, its name, or its trademarks in any publicity, marketing material, or customer list without Customer's prior written consent in each instance, and Customer may require removal of any existing reference at any time.

6. INDEMNIFICATION
Vendor will defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Vendor and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and will indemnify Customer against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Customer will defend and indemnify Vendor against third-party claims arising from Customer's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

7. FEES AND PAYMENT
All fees are due and payable within 45 days after the invoice date. Amounts remaining unpaid past the due date bear interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month and the highest rate allowed by law. Customer is responsible for all applicable taxes, excluding taxes measured by Vendor's income.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement, and each party consents to their jurisdiction.

9. AUDIT
No more than once in any 12 month period, and on at least 15 business days' written notice, Customer may audit Vendor's records directly relating to the fees charged under this Agreement, at Customer's expense and during normal business hours. Audits do not include access to Vendor's systems, facilities used for other customers, or other customers' data. Delivery of Vendor's then-current SOC 2 Type II report satisfies Customer's audit rights for the applicable period.

10. WARRANTIES
Vendor warrants that the Services will materially conform to the Documentation for a period of 90 days following delivery. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance of the affected Services or, if re-performance fails, a refund of the fees paid for the affected Services. EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

11. SERVICES
Vendor will provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Customer will provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Vendor to perform.

12. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 12 months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 24-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

13. TERMINATION
Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 60 days' prior written notice. On such termination, Customer will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Prepaid fees are non-refundable. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice.

14. ASSIGNMENT
If Vendor undergoes a change of control, Customer may terminate this Agreement on written notice and Vendor will refund all prepaid fees, without limiting Customer's other remedies.

15. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for 3 years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

16. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

EXHIBIT B
SUPPORT TERMS

B-1. Support Channels. Vendor provides support through its support portal and by email during Support Hours. Priority 1 incidents may additionally be reported by telephone at the number published in the portal.

B-2. Severity Levels. Priority 1: the production Platform is unavailable or materially unusable with no Workaround. Priority 2: a material feature is impaired with significant business impact. Priority 3: a non-material impairment or question. Priority 4: cosmetic issues and enhancement requests.

B-3. Response Targets. During Support Hours, Vendor targets initial response within 30 minutes for Priority 1, four (4) Support Hours for Priority 2, one (1) Business Day for Priority 3, and three (3) Business Days for Priority 4.

B-4. Escalation. If an incident is not progressing in line with its severity, Customer may request escalation through the portal; escalations are reviewed by Vendor's support management within one (1) Business Day.

B-5. Customer Responsibilities. Customer will provide information reasonably necessary to reproduce and diagnose reported issues, maintain current contact points, and apply Updates made available by Vendor within a reasonable period.

B-6. Exclusions from Support. Support does not cover issues caused by modifications not made by Vendor, Third-Party Materials, use contrary to the Documentation, or environments that do not meet the system requirements in the Documentation.


EXHIBIT C
ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

C-1. Prohibited Content. Authorized Users may not submit content to the Services that is unlawful, infringing, or that they lack the rights to submit, or that contains Malicious Code.

C-2. Prohibited Conduct. Authorized Users may not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform without written authorization; interfere with service to any user; or attempt to access accounts or data they are not authorized to access.

C-3. Usage Integrity. Credentials are per-individual and may not be shared. Automated access must use the published APIs and respect documented rate limits.

C-4. Enforcement. Vendor may suspend access that violates this policy where continued access poses a risk to the Services or other customers, with notice as provided in the Agreement, and will restore access promptly once the violation is cured.
