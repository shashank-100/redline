MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — Pinnfold Health DRAFT (counterparty paper)
============================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of July 17, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Pinnfold Health ("Customer") and Corvessa Labs, Inc. ("Provider"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the jurisdiction of Customer's principal place of business.

"Change Order" means a written instrument executed by both parties modifying an Order Form or Statement of Work.

"Deliverable" means a work product identified as a deliverable in a Statement of Work.

"Force Majeure Event" means an event beyond a party's reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, utility failures, and governmental action.

"Malicious Code" means viruses, worms, time bombs, and other code, files, or programs designed to disrupt, damage, or limit the operation of software or hardware.

"Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person contained in Customer Data.

"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform, including its tools, interfaces, and infrastructure.

"Services" means the Platform subscriptions and Professional Services provided under this Agreement.

"Statement of Work" means a document describing Professional Services executed by both parties that references this Agreement.

"Usage Data" means technical logs, telemetry, and metadata generated by operation of the Platform, excluding the content of Customer Data.

2. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may transfer this Agreement, by assignment, operation of law, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, except that Provider may do so without consent to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets. A purported transfer in breach of this Section is void.

3. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
For each calendar month in which the Services fail to meet the availability targets in the applicable order, Provider shall pay Customer, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of the monthly fees, without limiting Customer's other remedies.

4. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and shall indemnify Customer against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Customer shall defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

5. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement runs 12 months from the Effective Date. Any renewal, including pricing for the renewal period, requires a writing signed by both parties.

6. SERVICES
Provider shall provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Customer shall provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Provider to perform.

7. NON-SOLICITATION
Neither party shall, during the term of this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, directly solicit for hire any employee of the other party with whom it worked under this Agreement. Postings addressed to the general public, and hires resulting from them, do not violate this Section.

8. WARRANTIES
Provider warrants on a continuing basis throughout the term that the Services shall conform to the Documentation and shall be available without material interruption. Customer may pursue any remedy at law or in equity for breach of this warranty.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the law of the State of Delaware, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in Delaware.

10. TERMINATION
Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 30 days' prior written notice. On such termination, Customer shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Provider shall refund a prorated portion of any prepaid fees covering the period after the effective date of termination. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and shall use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for 3 years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

12. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All software, tools, methodologies, and other work product used or developed in connection with the Services, including the underlying platform and all improvements to it, are works made for hire and are hereby assigned to, and jointly owned by, Customer.

13. FEES AND PAYMENT
Customer shall pay all undisputed fees within 15 days of the date of each invoice. Amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Customer is responsible for all taxes arising from its purchases under this Agreement, excluding taxes on Provider's net income.

14. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

EXHIBIT B
SUPPORT TERMS

B-1. Support Channels. Provider provides support through its support portal and by email during Support Hours. Priority 1 incidents may additionally be reported by telephone at the number published in the portal.

B-2. Severity Levels. Priority 1: the production Platform is unavailable or materially unusable with no Workaround. Priority 2: a material feature is impaired with significant business impact. Priority 3: a non-material impairment or question. Priority 4: cosmetic issues and enhancement requests.

B-3. Response Targets. During Support Hours, Provider targets initial response within 60 minutes for Priority 1, four (4) Support Hours for Priority 2, one (1) Business Day for Priority 3, and three (3) Business Days for Priority 4.

B-4. Escalation. If an incident is not progressing in line with its severity, Customer may request escalation through the portal; escalations are reviewed by Provider's support management within one (1) Business Day.

B-5. Customer Responsibilities. Customer will provide information reasonably necessary to reproduce and diagnose reported issues, maintain current contact points, and apply Updates made available by Provider within a reasonable period.

B-6. Exclusions from Support. Support does not cover issues caused by modifications not made by Provider, Third-Party Materials, use contrary to the Documentation, or environments that do not meet the system requirements in the Documentation.


EXHIBIT C
ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

C-1. Prohibited Content. Authorized Users may not submit content to the Services that is unlawful, infringing, or that they lack the rights to submit, or that contains Malicious Code.

C-2. Prohibited Conduct. Authorized Users may not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform without written authorization; interfere with service to any user; or attempt to access accounts or data they are not authorized to access.

C-3. Usage Integrity. Credentials are per-individual and may not be shared. Automated access must use the published APIs and respect documented rate limits.

C-4. Enforcement. Provider may suspend access that violates this policy where continued access poses a risk to the Services or other customers, with notice as provided in the Agreement, and will restore access promptly once the violation is cured.
