MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — Aldermere Logistics DRAFT (counterparty paper)
============================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of July 6, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Aldermere Logistics ("Client") and Corvessa Labs, Inc. ("Supplier"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Affiliate" means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests.

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the jurisdiction of Client's principal place of business.

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, moral rights, and all other intellectual property rights, registered or unregistered, worldwide.

"Order Form" means an ordering document for subscriptions to the Services executed by both parties that references this Agreement.

"Platform" means Supplier's proprietary software-as-a-service platform, including its tools, interfaces, and infrastructure.

"Services" means the Platform subscriptions and Professional Services provided under this Agreement.

"Statement of Work" means a document describing Professional Services executed by both parties that references this Agreement.

"Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Supplier to process Customer Data in connection with the Services.

"Taxes" means sales, use, value-added, withholding, and similar taxes, however designated, other than taxes on Supplier's net income.

"Usage Data" means technical logs, telemetry, and metadata generated by operation of the Platform, excluding the content of Customer Data.

2. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement without cause upon sixty (60) days' advance written notice. Fees accrue through the effective date of termination, and prepaid amounts are not returnable. Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client will pay all undisputed fees within fifteen (15) days of the date of each invoice. Amounts not paid when due will accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Client is responsible for all taxes arising from its purchases under this Agreement, excluding taxes on Supplier's net income.

4. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may transfer this Agreement, by assignment, operation of law, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, except that Supplier may do so without consent to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets. A purported transfer in breach of this Section is void.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of it.

6. INSURANCE
Supplier will maintain, at its expense, commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, technology errors and omissions coverage of at least $5,000,000, and cyber liability coverage of at least $10,000,000, in each case naming Client as an additional insured, and will provide certificates of insurance on request.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR OWED BY CLIENT DURING THE EIGHTEEN (18) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY, AND NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR INDIRECT DAMAGES. This limitation does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 12 (Confidentiality), Client's payment obligations, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement runs twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, after which it extends automatically for successive periods of twenty-four (24) months each unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current period.

9. WARRANTIES
Supplier warrants that the Services will materially conform to the Documentation for a period of ninety (90) days following delivery. Client's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance of the affected Services or, if re-performance fails, a refund of the fees paid for the affected Services. EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND SUPPLIER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

10. AUDIT
Client may examine Supplier's records that directly support the fees invoiced under this Agreement and Supplier's compliance with it up to twice in any twelve (12) month period where and to the extent Client's regulators require, and otherwise once in any such period, on no less than fifteen (15) business days' prior notice, during business hours and at Client's cost. No audit extends to Supplier's systems or to materials of Supplier's other customers. A current SOC 2 Type II report from Supplier discharges this right for the period the report covers.

11. SERVICES
Supplier will provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Client will provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Supplier to perform.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

13. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Supplier and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, the Services, the Documentation, and all improvements, modifications, and derivative works of any of them. Client receives no rights except the subscriptions and licenses expressly granted. Client hereby assigns to Supplier all right, title, and interest in any feedback or suggestions relating to the Platform or Services.

14. NON-SOLICITATION
Neither party will, during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after it ends, directly solicit for hire any employee of the other party with whom it worked under this Agreement. Postings addressed to the general public, and hires resulting from them, do not violate this Section.

15. INDEMNIFICATION
Supplier will defend Client against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Supplier and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and will indemnify Client against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Client will defend and indemnify Supplier against third-party claims arising from Client's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

16. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
