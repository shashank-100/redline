This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of August 12, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Drummond-Weir Industries ("Customer") and Corvessa Labs, Inc. ("Provider"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Affiliate" means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests.

"Aggregated Data" means data derived from Customer's use of the Services that has been aggregated with other data such that it does not identify Customer or any natural person.

"Authorized User" means an employee or contractor of Customer or its Affiliates whom Customer permits to access the Services under this Agreement.

"Beta Service" means a service or feature identified as alpha, beta, preview, early access, or evaluation, made available at no additional charge.

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the jurisdiction of Customer's principal place of business.

"Change Order" means a written instrument executed by both parties modifying an Order Form or Statement of Work.

"Customer Data" means electronic data submitted by or on behalf of Customer to the Services, excluding Aggregated Data and Usage Data.

"Deliverable" means a work product identified as a deliverable in a Statement of Work.

"Documentation" means Provider's then-current usage documentation for the Platform, as updated from time to time.

"Effective Date" means the date identified as such in the preamble of this Agreement.

"Error" means a reproducible failure of the Platform to conform in material respects to the Documentation.

"Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under an Order Form or Statement of Work, exclusive of taxes.

"Force Majeure Event" means an event beyond a party's reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, utility failures, and governmental action.

"Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, moral rights, and all other intellectual property rights, registered or unregistered, worldwide.

"Malicious Code" means viruses, worms, time bombs, and other code, files, or programs designed to disrupt, damage, or limit the operation of software or hardware.

"Order Form" means an ordering document for subscriptions to the Services executed by both parties that references this Agreement.

"Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person contained in Customer Data.

"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform, including its tools, interfaces, and infrastructure.

"Professional Services" means implementation, configuration, migration, training, or advisory services described in a Statement of Work.

"Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance performed during the windows published in the Documentation or notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

"Security Incident" means a confirmed accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Customer Data.

"Services" means the Platform subscriptions and Professional Services provided under this Agreement.

"Statement of Work" means a document describing Professional Services executed by both parties that references this Agreement.

"Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Provider to process Customer Data in connection with the Services.

"Subscription Term" means the period of authorized access to the Platform stated in the applicable Order Form.

"Support Hours" means the hours during which Provider provides support, as set out in the Support Terms.

"Taxes" means sales, use, value-added, withholding, and similar taxes, however designated, other than taxes on Provider's net income.

"Third-Party Materials" means software, data, or services provided by third parties and identified as such in the Documentation or an Order Form.

"Update" means a modification to the Platform made generally available by Provider to its customers at no additional charge.

"Usage Data" means technical logs, telemetry, and metadata generated by operation of the Platform, excluding the content of Customer Data.

"Uptime Percentage" means the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the production Platform is available, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure Events.

"Workaround" means a change in procedures or data that avoids the practical effect of an Error without materially impairing use of the Platform.

Provider may suspend access to the Services where Customer's use presents a security risk to the Services or other customers, where required by law, or where undisputed fees are more than thirty (30) days overdue after notice; Provider will limit any suspension in scope and duration to what is reasonably necessary and will restore access promptly once the cause is resolved.

7. ANTI-CORRUPTION
Each party will comply with applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws and has not received or been offered any illegal or improper bribe, kickback, or payment in connection with this Agreement.

8. AUDIT

11. TERMINATION

On such termination, Customer will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Provider will refund a prorated portion of any prepaid fees covering the period after the effective date of termination. EXHIBIT D
SECURITY ADDENDUM

D-1. Security Program. Provider maintains a written information security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Customer Data, aligned to recognized industry frameworks, and reviewed at least annually.

D-2. Personnel. Provider personnel with access to Customer Data are bound by confidentiality obligations, receive security training on hire and annually thereafter, and are subject to background screening where permitted by law.

D-3. Access Control. Access to Customer Data is restricted to personnel who require it to perform the Services, is provisioned on least-privilege principles, requires multi-factor authentication, and is reviewed at least quarterly.

D-4. Encryption. Customer Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent algorithm. Encryption keys are managed through a dedicated key management service with rotation on a defined schedule.

D-5. Network Security. Production environments are segmented from corporate networks, protected by firewalls and intrusion detection, and subject to continuous vulnerability scanning with remediation on defined timelines by severity.

D-6. Development Practices. Provider follows a secure development lifecycle including peer code review, dependency scanning, static analysis, and separation of production and non-production environments. Production Customer Data is not used in non-production environments.

D-7. Penetration Testing. Provider engages an independent third party to perform penetration testing of the Platform at least annually and remediates findings on timelines commensurate with risk. A summary attestation is available on request.

D-8. Incident Response Plan. Provider maintains and tests an incident response plan. Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after confirming a Security Incident affecting Customer Data, and will provide information reasonably requested to support Customer's obligations.

D-9. Business Continuity. Provider maintains business continuity and disaster recovery plans for the production Platform, with a recovery point objective of 4 hours and a recovery time objective of 12 hours, tested at least annually.

D-10. Backups. Customer Data in the production Platform is backed up on a defined schedule; backups are encrypted, stored in geographically separate facilities, and tested for restorability.

D-11. Data Deletion. Within 60 days after termination or expiration of the Agreement, Provider will delete or render unrecoverable Customer Data in the production Platform, and thereafter in backups in the ordinary rotation cycle, except where retention is required by law.

D-12. Physical Security. Production infrastructure is hosted in data centers maintaining SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestations, with controlled access, monitoring, and environmental protections operated by the hosting provider.

D-13. Certifications. Provider maintains a SOC 2 Type II attestation covering the Platform and will provide the then-current report to Customer not more than once annually under confidentiality.

D-14. Subcontractor Security. Provider imposes security obligations on Subprocessors that are no less protective in material respects than this Addendum and remains responsible for their performance.

This Statement of Work is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between Corvessa Labs, Inc. ("Provider") and Drummond-Weir Industries ("Customer").

1. Services and scope. Provider will perform migration of legacy records identified in the data inventory, including validation reconciliation.
   The engagement further includes:
   - construction of the interfaces enumerated in the integration register, with error handling and replay
   - documentation of configured workflows and operational runbooks
   - performance validation against the agreed volumetrics baseline
   - integration with Customer's identity provider for single sign-on and SCIM provisioning
   - hypercare support for the period stated in the milestone table
   - administrator enablement sessions and recorded train-the-trainer materials
   - role and permission model implementation to the approved matrix
   - cutover plan authorship, dress rehearsal, and go-live execution support
   - configuration of scheduled data extracts to Customer's warehouse landing zone

2. Milestones and payment.
  M1  Discovery complete  —  week 2  —  USD 96,000
  M2  Design approved  —  week 5  —  USD 48,000
  M3  Configuration complete  —  week 8  —  USD 144,000
  M4  Integrations verified  —  week 11  —  USD 48,000
  M5  Migration validated  —  week 14  —  USD 144,000
  M6  UAT exit  —  week 17  —  USD 144,000

3. Deliverables.
   D1. Migration of legacy records identified in the data inventory, including validation reconciliation. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. The Deliverable includes a summary of decisions taken and open risks.
   D2. Construction of the interfaces enumerated in the integration register, with error handling and replay. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. Source materials are provided in the formats stated in the design document.
   D3. Documentation of configured workflows and operational runbooks. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. Interim drafts are shared for review at the checkpoints in the project plan.
   D4. Performance validation against the agreed volumetrics baseline. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. The Deliverable includes a summary of decisions taken and open risks.
   D5. Integration with Customer's identity provider for single sign-on and SCIM provisioning. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. The Deliverable includes a summary of decisions taken and open risks.
   D6. Hypercare support for the period stated in the milestone table. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. Source materials are provided in the formats stated in the design document.
   D7. Administrator enablement sessions and recorded train-the-trainer materials. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. The Deliverable includes a summary of decisions taken and open risks.
   D8. Role and permission model implementation to the approved matrix. The Deliverable for this item comprises the artifacts, configuration, and documentation produced in its performance, delivered per the project plan. Source materials are provided in the formats stated in the design document.

4. Acceptance. Customer will review each Deliverable within ten (10) Business Days of delivery and either accept it or provide a consolidated statement of material nonconformities with the Statement of Work. Provider will correct material nonconformities and redeliver; a Deliverable is deemed accepted if no such statement is received within the review window, or on first productive use.

5. Personnel. Provider will staff the engagement with personnel qualified for their roles and may substitute personnel of equivalent qualification on notice. Named key personnel, if any, are listed in the project plan and will not be reassigned without Customer's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

6. Assumptions.
   - Third-party license costs, if any, are Customer's responsibility.
   - Source data is provided in the agreed formats no later than the dates in the project plan.
   - Changes to scope proceed only by Change Order.
   - Work is performed remotely; travel, if requested, is billed at cost with prior approval.
   - Customer provides timely access to subject-matter owners and source systems.
   - One consolidated round of feedback per Deliverable is assumed.

7. Change control. Changes to this Statement of Work are effective only by Change Order executed by both parties.

BF-1. Australia. Where Customer or its Affiliate purchases Services for deployment to Authorized Users located in Australia, the following additional terms apply. (a) Invoicing entity. Provider may invoice through its local Affiliate or branch, and amounts remain payable in the currency stated in the applicable Order Form. (b) Taxes. Amounts payable are exclusive of goods and services, value-added, and similar taxes imposed in Australia, which will be added to invoices at the applicable rate; each party is responsible for taxes on its own income. (c) Withholding. If Customer is required by the law of Australia to withhold from a payment, Customer will gross up the payment so Provider receives the amount it would have received absent the withholding, and will provide official receipts evidencing remittance. (d) Employment notices. Nothing in this Schedule creates an employment relationship between Provider and any Authorized User in Australia. (e) Local mandatory law. If a mandatory provision of the law of Australia applicable to Customer's use of the Services conflicts with this Schedule, the mandatory provision prevails to the extent of the conflict for that deployment only. (f) Data residency. Where an Order Form states a data residency election for Australia, Customer Data at rest for the covered workloads is stored in the elected region; telemetry and support artifacts may be processed elsewhere as described in the Documentation. (g) Support hours. Support Hours for Authorized Users in Australia follow the regional schedule published in the support portal, and Priority 1 incidents are handled on the follow-the-sun rotation regardless of region. (h) Notices. Operational notices for deployments in Australia may be given through the administrative console in addition to the notice methods stated in the Agreement.
